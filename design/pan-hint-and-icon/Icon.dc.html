Skip the redraw and ship the mark that already exists. It kills the download read outright, it is already proven at small sizes, and it costs nothing. Shipped as-is. The mark does not only sit next to FixedShot in the menu bar — it sits inside Longshot, in the popup header and the editor header, surrounded by #FF4F00. A cool tile there read as a foreign object, and the product a mark lives in is the one it has to match; the menu-bar comparison happens far less often than that. So the two apps now share one mark. If telling them apart ever matters more than theme consistency, the lever is the drawing rather than the colour — Seam on an orange tile changes nothing about the palette.
Outcome
D, as-is — FixedShot's viewfinder, FixedShot's colourway. It removes the download read, costs no new drawing, and is the only option that also matches the orange UI the icon is embedded in. Seam stays on the shelf as the version to reach for if the two apps ever need to be told apart: it is the one direction that differentiates without touching the palette.
