## [0.2.0] - 2026-08-21

### Added

- Full-page scrolling capture for documents and dominant inner scrollers.
- Crop, drawing, arrow, shape, text, pixelate, and magnifier tools.
- PNG and JPEG export, clipboard copy, and configurable output size.
- English and Simplified Chinese interfaces.
- Local-only processing with no analytics, accounts, or host permissions.
